Privacy Policy

Debug Toolkit · Last updated 24 September 2026

Debug Toolkit ("the app") is a network diagnostics utility for Android. This policy explains what data the app processes, what is sent to third parties, and the choices available to you. The app does not require an account or login, and it does not collect names, email addresses, or other identifying registration data.

Data the app stores on your device

The app stores the following locally, using Android's private app storage:

  • Your selected theme (light, dark, or system default)
  • Your analytics and cookie consent choice
  • Your acknowledgement of the Cloudflare network tools disclosure described below

None of this local data is transmitted anywhere by the app itself.

Data you choose to enter

Several tools in the app act on information you type in. This data is sent only to the destination you specify, in order to perform the requested check, and is not stored by the app beyond the current session:

ToolWhat you enterWhere it is sent
Certificate inspector A hostname Directly to that hostname, to read its public TLS certificate
DNS lookup A domain name and record type Directly to that domain's authoritative servers, or to Cloudflare's public DNS resolver (1.1.1.1) if you select it as the DNS provider
HTTP headers / response inspector A URL Directly to that URL, to read its response headers and status
Speed test No manual input Cloudflare's public speed test endpoints, to measure download, upload, and latency

Because these tools connect directly to servers you specify or to Cloudflare, those third parties receive your device's IP address and the request itself, subject to their own privacy practices. The app does not proxy, log, or retain the results once you leave the relevant screen.

Wi-Fi and network information

The network monitor reads Wi-Fi signal strength (dBm), link speed, and connection details using Android's system APIs, which may require location permission on some Android versions because Wi-Fi scan results can reveal nearby network identifiers. This information stays on your device and is only displayed to you.

Analytics, crash reporting, and performance monitoring

The app uses the following Firebase services, provided by Google, to help diagnose crashes and understand feature usage:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash and error diagnostics automatically, so issues can be fixed. This is not gated behind consent, as it does not include personal data or the content of the tools you run.
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring — collect anonymised usage events (such as which tool was opened) and performance timings. These are only enabled after you grant analytics consent in the app's settings, in line with EU cookie and consent requirements. You can withdraw consent at any time from the same screen, which disables further collection.

None of these services receive the domains, hostnames, or URLs you enter into the app's tools.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose technical utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data retention

Locally stored preferences persist until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Crash and analytics data collected by Firebase is retained by Google according to Firebase's own retention periods.

Your choices

  • Grant or withdraw analytics consent at any time from the app's settings screen.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored preferences.
  • Third-party destinations you query directly (certificate, DNS, and HTTP tools) are subject to that destination's own privacy policy, not this one.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app's features change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
